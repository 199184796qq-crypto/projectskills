# 镜头语言与光影色调

## 常用运镜

- **环绕镜头**：orbiting camera, 360-degree rotation around the subject
- **推进镜头**：camera slowly pushing in, dramatic zoom
- **航拍俯冲**：aerial shot diving down through clouds
- **跟随镜头**：camera following the character in flight
- **慢动作**：slow motion, time slows down
- **升格特写**：extreme close-up with slow motion
- **长镜头**：continuous long take, unbroken shot

## 景别选择

- 远景（wide shot）：展现仙山全貌、战场规模
- 中景（medium shot）：角色施法、对话
- 近景（close-up）：表情、手印、法器细节
- 特写（extreme close-up）：眼睛变色、灵力流转

## 仙侠色调方案

- **仙境调**：soft golden light, ethereal glow, warm highlights, misty atmosphere
- **冰寒调**：cool blue tones, frost particles, pale moonlight, icy reflections
- **魔域调**：deep crimson and black, ominous glow, dark purple shadows
- **黄昏调**：warm orange sunset, long shadows, golden hour lighting
- **月夜调**：silver moonlight, cool blue shadows, luminous spiritual energy
