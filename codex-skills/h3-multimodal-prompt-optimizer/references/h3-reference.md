# H3 Full-Reference 参考速查

## 固定输出骨架

```text
subject_definitions:
<Subject 1> is ...
<Picture 1> is ...
<Video 1> is ...
<Audio 1> is ...

summary:
[reference generation] The target video ...

retention_analysis:
<Subject 1> (appears in [Shot 1]): fully_preserved - ...

detailed_description:
The target video uses ...
[Shot 1] ...
[Shot 2] At 00:03.000, ...

overall_soundscape:
...

non_diegetic_music:
...
```

## 标签职责

| 标签 | 建立条件 | 典型写法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `<Subject N>` | 目标画面实际复用的人、物、场景、服装、动作或风格 | `<Subject 1> is the woman whose identity comes from <Picture 1> and whose walk comes from <Video 1>.` |
| `<Picture N>` | 图片本身是帧或构图锚点 | `<Picture 2> is the final-frame composition anchor for [Shot 2].` |
| `<Video N>` | 源视频编辑、续写或整体镜头/节奏参考 | `<Video 1> is the source video whose camera path guides the target video.` |
| `<Audio N>` | 音频复制或明确的音色、歌词、音乐、音效参考 | `<Audio 1> is the voice-timbre reference for <Subject 1> (S1).` |

## 运镜句式

- `The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed toward <Subject 1>'s eyes as her guarded expression gradually softens.`
- `The camera tracks backward at matching speed with stable eye-level framing, preserving facial identity and background direction.`
- `The camera performs a measured arc around <Subject 2>, preserving the garment silhouette and logo while foreground reflections create natural parallax.`
- `The camera remains static while the subject enters frame and settles into a balanced three-quarter composition.`

## 动作因果句式

`<Subject 1> shifts her weight onto the supporting leg before stepping forward; heel contact precedes the foot roll to the toe, followed by restrained counter-motion in the pelvis, shoulders, arms, hair, and garment.`

`Her fingertips make contact before the clasp rotates; the metal component resists briefly, clicks into place, and stops without overshoot. The click occurs exactly at the locking moment.`

## 表情与视线句式

`Her gaze initially rests slightly below the lens. After recognizing the off-screen target, her eyes lift first and hold a precise focus point; her head follows with a subtle delay, her brows tighten, her jaw steadies, and one measured breath shifts her expression toward quiet resolve.`

## 灯光与材质句式

`A large diffused 4300K key light from camera-left shapes the face and garment, while a restrained cooler fill preserves shadow detail and a narrow warm rim separates the subject from the background. Catchlights, shadows, reflections, and material highlights shift consistently with subject and camera movement.`

`Silk shows directional soft highlights that glide along the folds; metal carries coherent environmental reflections; glass exhibits transmission, refraction, and angle-dependent Fresnel reflections; wet ground reflects the actual practical lights.`

## 一致性锚点

`The character's facial identity, hairstyle, body proportions, wardrobe, accessories, and left-right details remain stable throughout the shot.`

`<Subject 2>'s exact geometry, dimensions, surface finish, controls, label text, logo placement, and left-right orientation remain fully preserved across every angle. The product does not warp, duplicate, resize, or change construction.`
